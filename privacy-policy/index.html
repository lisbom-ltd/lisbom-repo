1 Who we are

LisBom Limited ("LisBom", "we", "us", "our") is a company registered in England & Wales. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website and in the course of our services.

We connect UK and international businesses with Filipino professionals, managing recruitment, onboarding, and ongoing employment. This means we handle personal data relating to two groups of people: business clients and their contacts, and candidates and placed talent (primarily based in the Philippines).

Questions about this policy? Contact us at support@lisbom-ltd.com.

4 International data transfers

Our core service involves transferring personal data between the UK and the Philippines. The Philippines is not currently on the UK's list of countries with an adequacy decision, which means we must put appropriate safeguards in place.

We rely on the following transfer mechanisms:

  • UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs) — standard contractual clauses approved by the ICO, incorporated into our agreements with candidates, clients, and sub-processors in the Philippines
  • Legitimate interests assessment — for transfers necessary to fulfil our contractual obligations to clients

In addition, the Philippines has its own data protection law — the Data Privacy Act of 2012 — which imposes obligations on any party that processes Philippine residents' personal data. We comply with both UK GDPR and this Act.

If you are a candidate based in the Philippines, your personal data will be shared with UK-based clients as part of our service. We ensure all clients are bound by appropriate data protection obligations before any transfer takes place.

6 Who we share your data with

We do not sell your data. We only share personal data where necessary:

  • Client businesses — candidate profiles are shared with prospective clients as part of our placement service
  • Payroll and HR tools — used to process salaries and manage employment records
  • Email and CRM platforms — to manage client and candidate communications (e.g. HubSpot, Google Workspace)
  • Calendly — to facilitate discovery call bookings; their privacy policy applies to data you enter on their platform
  • Analytics providers — such as Google Analytics, where you have consented to analytics cookies
  • Legal or regulatory authorities — where required by law (e.g. HMRC, ICO)

All third-party processors are bound by data processing agreements and may only process your data on our instructions.

8 Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — ask us to delete your data where there is no compelling reason for us to continue processing it
  • Right to restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Rights related to automated decision-making — we do not make solely automated decisions that significantly affect you

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@lisbom-ltd.com. We will respond within one calendar month. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing a request.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal exemptions in certain circumstances.
