LisBom vs Deel

Deel and LisBom solve different problems, and the honest answer is that the right one depends on what you actually need. Here's a fact-checked, no-spin comparison to help you decide.

They're not really competing for the same job

Deel is a global HR, payroll and Employer of Record (EOR) platform. It helps companies that already know who they want to hire legally employ and pay that person in over 150 countries, handling contracts, statutory compliance and payroll. It's software plus compliance infrastructure - it does not find, vet or shortlist candidates for you.

LisBom is a staffing agency that specializes in one country: the Philippines. We find, vet and place a dedicated Filipino professional in roles like virtual assistant, customer support, bookkeeping, social media management and SEO, then manage onboarding and ongoing support. There's no software for you to run and no per-country compliance fee, because we only operate in one market and know it well.

If you need a person sourced, vetted and placed, LisBom is built for that. If you already have specific hires across many countries and need employment infrastructure to legally pay them, Deel is built for that. Below is a side-by-side so you can judge fit rather than take our word for it.

Common questions

Is Deel a staffing agency like LisBom?

No. Deel is a global HR, payroll and Employer of Record (EOR) software platform that helps companies legally employ and pay staff in 150+ countries. It does not source, vet, or shortlist candidates for you. LisBom is a staffing agency that finds, vets, and places a dedicated Filipino professional for your business, with onboarding and ongoing support included.

How much does Deel cost compared to LisBom?

Deel's published Employer of Record pricing starts at $599 per employee per month as a platform fee, on top of salary and statutory employer costs. LisBom's placements start from £800/month, fully managed, with no separate platform or per-country compliance fee, since we specialize only in Philippines-based hiring.

When does it make more sense to use Deel instead of LisBom?

Deel is a strong fit if you already have a specific hire in mind and need multi-country legal employment infrastructure, or if you're hiring across many different countries at once. LisBom is built specifically for businesses that want help finding and managing dedicated Filipino talent, without running a software platform themselves.
