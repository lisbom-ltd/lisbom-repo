VA vs Full-Time Employee: The Real Cost Comparison for UK Businesses

By Diven Babu, LisBom
June 2026
5 min read

The salary figure on a job advert is not what a UK employee costs you. Not even close. By the time you have added up employer National Insurance, pension contributions, holiday pay, sick pay, equipment and recruitment fees, the real number is significantly higher - and most business owners only find this out after they have signed the contract.

This article shows you the actual all-in cost of a UK full-time hire versus a remote Filipino professional through LisBom. No spin. Just the numbers.

The True Cost of a UK Full-Time Employee

Key takeaway: A £28,000 salary job costs you closer to £36,000 per year in reality - before the person has delivered a single outcome.
