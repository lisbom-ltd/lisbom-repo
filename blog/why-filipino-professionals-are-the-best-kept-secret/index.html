Why Filipino Professionals Are the World's Best-Kept Secret for Remote Hiring

By Diven Babu, LisBom
June 2026
6 min read

Every business owner I speak to asks about cost first. It is the obvious question. And the honest answer - 60-70% savings versus a UK equivalent hire - tends to end that part of the conversation pretty quickly.

But here is what does not get talked about nearly enough: the cost saving is almost secondary once you have worked with a Filipino professional. What actually keeps businesses coming back, scaling their teams, and referring others is something harder to put a number on.

This article is about that. The full picture - not just the economics.

The Language Advantage Goes Deeper Than You Think

The Philippines is ranked second in Asia for English proficiency. But that statistic alone does not capture what it means in practice.

English is not a second language in the Philippines - it is a co-official language. It is used daily in business, law, education, government and media. Filipino professionals grow up writing and speaking it in professional contexts, not just learning it for work.

What this means for you: no translation layer. No communication overhead. No misunderstandings from cultural idiom or tone. When you ask for something, you get back exactly what you asked for - communicated clearly, professionally, and often with questions that show the person actually understood the brief.

This is not the case everywhere you might look for remote talent. It is a genuine differentiator.

Cultural Alignment With Western Business

The Philippines has a deep historical connection with Western culture - particularly American and British business norms. This is not about losing Filipino identity; it is about genuine familiarity with how UK, US and Australian businesses operate.

Filipino professionals understand workplace hierarchy, professional email etiquette, client-facing communication standards, and the concept of ownership over a role. They are not waiting to be micromanaged. They take initiative, flag problems early, and genuinely care about the outcome.

"Every placement we make creates a life-changing opportunity for a Filipino professional - while giving businesses access to talent they could not otherwise afford. Both sides win. That is not a tagline. It is the whole point."

There is also something that is genuinely difficult to articulate without sounding like marketing copy: loyalty and commitment. Filipino professionals, when given meaningful work and treated with respect, invest themselves in it. They grow with you. The retention rates we see at LisBom are consistently higher than most UK hiring outcomes.

The Talent Pool Is Enormous - And Underutilised

Over 500,000 graduates enter the Philippine job market every year across finance, technology, marketing, healthcare and operations. The country has a mature, established business process outsourcing sector - the IT-BPM industry alone generates over $40 billion in annual revenue - which means the infrastructure for remote, professional work is already there.

These are not people learning to work remotely. They have done it for global companies for decades. The difference with LisBom is that instead of that talent going to large corporations, it goes directly to growing businesses like yours.

Time Zone Flexibility

One concern that comes up regularly: "Will they actually be available during our working hours?"

Filipino professionals are highly experienced at aligning their schedules to international clients. Whether you are UK-based (GMT), US-based (EST or PST) or in Australia (AEST), your LisBom team member works your hours. This is completely standard practice - not an exception or an extra cost.

For some businesses, this even creates an advantage. A customer support agent aligned to UK hours means your customers get responses during the business day without you needing to be the one answering.

The Bottom Line

The cost saving is real and it is significant. But businesses that hire through LisBom and stay with us long-term are not doing it purely because of the numbers. They are doing it because the quality is there, the communication is strong, and the people they hire become genuine long-term assets.

If you have been on the fence about remote hiring - wondering whether it is too good to be true, or whether the quality will really be there - I would say this: book a call. Ask the hard questions. We will answer them honestly, including if we think it is not the right fit for your business.
