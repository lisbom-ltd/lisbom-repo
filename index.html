Who's behind LisBom

Not a staffing agency.
A growth partner.

I'm Diven Babu. Born in Portugal, moved to London in 2016, and spent the next eight years inside financial planning and software businesses — analysing costs, modelling growth, and watching how companies actually operate under the hood.

What I kept seeing: talented, ambitious businesses grinding to a halt — not because of strategy or product, but because of time. Time eaten by admin. Processes that didn't need a London salary to get done brilliantly. Founders doing work that was holding them back from the work that mattered.

The answer came from the Philippines. I'd seen first-hand what Filipino professionals are capable of — genuine pride, loyalty, and a commitment to being part of something bigger than a job description. They don't clock in and clock out. They invest themselves. They grow with you.

That combination felt like something worth building around. A UK-managed bridge between businesses ready to scale and the talent to help them do it. So I built LisBom.

We're actively building our first UK client roster — which means founder-level attention on every placement, early-adopter pricing, and a partner with every reason to make yours exceptional.
