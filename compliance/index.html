Fix 1 — Working Cookie Consent Banner

The current "Cookie Settings" footer link does nothing (javascript:void(0)). UK PECR requires real, functioning consent before setting non-essential cookies. The simplest compliant solution is to use CookieYes (free tier available) or Cookiebot.

Option A — CookieYes (Recommended, Free Tier)

1
Sign up at cookieyes.com → Add your domain → Copy your embed script.
2
Paste the script tag into the <head> of every page on your site (before any analytics scripts):
<!-- CookieYes banner — paste your actual script URL from the CookieYes dashboard -->
<script id="cookieyes" type="text/javascript"
  src="https://cdn-cookieyes.com/client_data/YOUR_ID_HERE/script.js">
</script>
3
Replace the "Cookie Settings" footer link with the CookieYes re-open trigger:
<!-- BEFORE (broken) -->
<a href="javascript:void(0)">Cookie Settings</a>

<!-- AFTER (working) -->
<a href="javascript:void(0)" class="cky-banner-element">Cookie Settings</a>
4
In the CookieYes dashboard, scan your site to auto-detect cookies, then categorise them (Google Analytics → Analytics; Calendly → Functional). This populates your cookie declaration automatically.
✅ CookieYes free tier covers up to 25,000 page views/month and is ICO-compliant. It also auto-blocks Google Analytics until consent is given.

Option B — Move Google Analytics to Consent-Gated Loading (Manual)

If you prefer not to use a third-party banner tool, you can load GA only after consent. Replace your current GA snippet with:

<!-- Consent-gated Google Analytics -->
<script>
  window.dataLayer = window.dataLayer || [];
  function gtag(){ dataLayer.push(arguments); }

  // Default: deny all non-essential cookies
  gtag('consent', 'default', {
    analytics_storage: 'denied',
    functionality_storage: 'denied',
    wait_for_update: 500
  });
</script>

<!-- Load GA tag AFTER consent default is set -->
<script async src="https://www.googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=G-XXXXXXXXXX"></script>
<script>
  gtag('js', new Date());
  gtag('config', 'G-XXXXXXXXXX');
</script>

<!-- Simple consent banner (inline) -->
<div id="cookie-banner" style="
  position:fixed; bottom:0; left:0; right:0; z-index:9999;
  background:#1a1a2e; color:#fff; padding:1rem 1.5rem;
  display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:space-between;
  flex-wrap:wrap; gap:0.75rem; font-size:0.875rem;
  box-shadow: 0 -2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.2);">
  <span>
    We use cookies to understand how visitors use our site.
    <a href="/privacy-policy/" style="color:#a5b4fc;">Learn more</a>
  </span>
  <div style="display:flex; gap:0.5rem;">
    <button onclick="setCookieConsent('denied')" style="
      background:transparent; border:1px solid #666; color:#ccc;
      padding:0.4rem 0.9rem; border-radius:6px; cursor:pointer; font-size:0.85rem;">
      Decline
    </button>
    <button onclick="setCookieConsent('granted')" style="
      background:#4f46e5; border:none; color:#fff;
      padding:0.4rem 0.9rem; border-radius:6px; cursor:pointer; font-size:0.85rem;">
      Accept All
    </button>
  </div>
</div>

<script>
  // Check existing preference
  const savedConsent = localStorage.getItem('cookie_consent');
  if (savedConsent) {
    document.getElementById('cookie-banner').style.display = 'none';
    gtag('consent', 'update', {
      analytics_storage: savedConsent,
      functionality_storage: savedConsent
    });
  }

  function setCookieConsent(value) {
    localStorage.setItem('cookie_consent', value);
    gtag('consent', 'update', {
      analytics_storage: value,
      functionality_storage: value
    });
    document.getElementById('cookie-banner').style.display = 'none';
  }

  // Re-open from footer link
  function openCookieSettings() {
    document.getElementById('cookie-banner').style.display = 'flex';
  }
</script>

<!-- Then update your footer link to: -->
<a href="javascript:openCookieSettings()">Cookie Settings</a>
⚠️ Option B (manual) is functional but basic. Option A (CookieYes) is easier to maintain and gives you an auto-generated cookie declaration table — which your privacy policy references.

Fix 2 — Privacy Notice on Careers / Application Forms

Under UK GDPR Article 13, you must provide privacy information at the point of data collection. Your contact form already does this well — your careers/application forms don't. Add the snippet below directly above each "Submit Application" button on careers.html.

For the General Application form and each role modal — paste above the submit button:

<!-- ✅ UK GDPR Article 13 transparency notice — add above every Submit button on careers.html -->
<p class="form-privacy-notice" style="
  font-size: 0.8rem;
  color: #666;
  margin-top: 1rem;
  padding: 0.75rem 1rem;
  background: #f8f7ff;
  border-radius: 6px;
  line-height: 1.5;">
  🔒 By submitting this application, you agree to LisBom Limited processing your personal data
  (including your name, email address, CV, and work history) for the purpose of assessing your
  suitability for this and future roles. We rely on our
  <strong>legitimate interests</strong> as the lawful basis for processing. We will retain your
  application data for up to <strong>6 months</strong> from the date we make a hiring decision,
  after which it will be securely deleted — unless you give us explicit consent to retain it
  for future opportunities. Your data will not be sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
  See our <a href="/privacy-policy/" style="color:#4f46e5;">Privacy Policy</a> for your full
  rights, including the right to access or delete your data.
</p>
✅ This covers the Article 13 requirements: identity of controller, purpose, lawful basis, retention period, and rights. It matches what your Privacy Policy already says about candidate data.

Fix 3 — Terms & Conditions Page

A full Terms & Conditions document has been created and provided as a separate file: lisbom-terms-and-conditions.html

1

<!-- BEFORE -->

Fix 4 — "Book a Call" Broken Links

All "Book a Call" / "Book a Discovery Call" buttons currently link to <> (empty href). If you're using Calendly, replace with one of the following options:

Option A — Direct Calendly Link (simplest)

<!-- Replace all href="<>" with your actual Calendly URL -->
<a href="https://calendly.com/YOUR_USERNAME/discovery-call" target="_blank"
   rel="noopener noreferrer">
  Book a Discovery Call
</a>

Option B — Inline Calendly Popup Widget

<!-- 1. Add to your <head> (consent-gated — only load after Functional cookies accepted) -->
<link href="https://assets.calendly.com/assets/external/widget.css" rel="stylesheet">
<script src="https://assets.calendly.com/assets/external/widget.js" type="text/javascript" async></script>

<!-- 2. Replace button/link onclick -->
<a href="javascript:void(0)"
   onclick="Calendly.initPopupWidget({url: 'https://calendly.com/YOUR_USERNAME/discovery-call'}); return false;">
  Book a Discovery Call
</a>
✅ Your privacy policy already mentions Calendly in Section 6 — so once the link is working, the data disclosure is covered. Just make sure the Functional cookie category in your banner covers Calendly.
